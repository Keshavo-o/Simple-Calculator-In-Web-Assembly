Why repeat the operation?

A single addition is too small to produce a useful timing comparison because browser timing overhead can be larger than the computation itself. Repeating the operation many times makes the measured difference easier to observe.

Where WebAssembly becomes more interesting

WebAssembly is designed for workloads where a large amount of computation is performed repeatedly. Examples include image processing, audio/video work, simulations, cryptography, and other performance-sensitive tasks.
